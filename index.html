Common pathways across the UK

Education is devolved, so England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland have differences in stages and qualifications. The outline below focuses mainly on England's Key Stage model.

Stage (England)Typical agesHow we can help
Early YearsUp to age 5Early literacy, numeracy and learning confidence.
Key Stage 15-7Reading, writing and foundational maths.
Key Stage 27-11Core subjects, homework and preparation for transition.
Key Stage 311-14Subject foundations and independent study habits.
Key Stage 414-16GCSE subject knowledge, revision and exam technique.
Post-1616-18A levels and other appropriate senior qualifications.
Scotland commonly uses Curriculum for Excellence and National Qualifications; Wales and Northern Ireland also have their own curriculum and qualification arrangements.

About The4Tutors

Helping students learn with confidence, one lesson at a time.

The4Tutors is a global online tutoring service focused on making personalised academic support approachable, engaging and accessible for families.

A friendly partner in your student's progress.

We believe learning improves when students feel comfortable enough to ask questions, practise without pressure and see how ideas connect. Our approach centres on one-to-one sessions, clear learning goals and teaching that adapts to the individual.

We support learners from kindergarten through school and into university-level study, including homework help, subject revision, exam preparation and study skills. Families can access lessons from home, making extra academic support easier to fit into everyday life.

For students with ADHD or autism, we aim to connect families with tutors who have relevant experience and can discuss suitable approaches before lessons begin. Each student's needs are different, and matching should be based on an individual conversation.

Payment policy

Clear expectations before lessons begin.

We aim to make our payment terms simple, fair and transparent for families.

  • The trial class is always free of charge.
  • Payments are accepted on a weekly or monthly basis.
  • Payment must be made before the first class of the selected week or month.
  • Payment frequency (weekly or monthly) may only be changed when no dues are outstanding.
  • All payments are required in advance to maintain a safe, focused and peaceful learning environment.
  • Orphans receive a 50% discount upon approval by the board.
  • Tactics such as claiming that payment has been sent and will be received soon in order to delay payment are strictly condemned.
  • The4Tutors reserves the right to suspend class enrollment in the event of repeated or serious payment delays.
